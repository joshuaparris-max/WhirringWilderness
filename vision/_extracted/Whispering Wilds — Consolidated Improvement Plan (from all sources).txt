What’s already strong (baseline to keep)
Robust UI scaffolding: side panels (Map/Quests/Inv/Help), command history, click-to-autofill, mini-map on move, toasts, save import/export, Dev Mode, mobile/desktop modes, settings persist.
Accessibility depth: profiles by age, guidance levels, safety toggles, dyslexic font, read-aloud, parental lock, simplified text, sliders for difficulty.
Gameplay spine: branching quests, bestiary with traits, weighted gathering by biome, sense cooldown, micro-goals with XP toasts, basic combat with stun/bleed tags.
