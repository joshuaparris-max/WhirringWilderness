Summary of Previous Feedback and Corresponding Fixes
What Jayden Liked:
Engaging gameplay
 – held his attention for more than 30 minutes.
Map command
 – very helpful for navigating.
Command buttons
 – quick access buttons made play easier.
Quality-of-Life Suggestions:
Better scrolling behavior
 – auto-scroll with bottom text and a dedicated scroll bar instead of an ever-expanding page.
Clickable commands/items
 – clicking NPCs or items should auto-fill the appropriate command.
Pin panels/messages
 – pin map, quests, inventory, or help into separate columns for easy reference.
Up-arrow history & batch actions
 – ability to recall previous commands and batch commands (e.g., “forage 5”).
Clearer crafting list
 – show which items can be crafted.
Map auto-update
 – show map automatically after movement.
Custom command buttons
 – allow users to create their own quick command buttons.
